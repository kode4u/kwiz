# [8] A Course-Specific Agentic RAG Chatbot for IT Student Support: Architecture, Local Deployment, and Preliminary Evaluation
* **Authors:** H. V. Tran, P. V. Nguyen, T. T. N. Vu, H. P. Luong, & D.-N. Le
* **Venue & Year:** Next-Generation Computing Systems and Technologies (2026), 2(2), 21–34
* **Category:** `On-Premise University RAG`
* **Associated Full Paper PDF:** `Reference [8] (NGCST 2026)`

---

### ⚡ 1-Minute Executive Takeaway
> **Key Finding:** Confirms on-premise university RAG satisfies privacy and compliance, but underscores the need for hardware-efficient inference.

---

### 🎯 Core Research Objective
To design, deploy, and evaluate an on-premise agentic RAG chatbot for course-specific IT student tutoring at Hai Phong University.

---

### 🔬 Methodology & Architecture
Deployed an open-weight language model on a local institutional server, indexing syllabus and lecture documents to support student Q&A interactions.

---

### 📊 Key Empirical Findings & Evidence
Proved that local institutional deployments effectively resolve student data privacy concerns and institutional compliance mandates, but identified high server response latency during peak student usage hours.

---

### 🔗 Direct Relevance to Our Kwiz Paper
Cited in Section 2.3. While Tran et al. focused on conversational student Q&A, our study focuses on instructor-facing structured assessment generation, deterministic schema compilation, and concurrent batch generation.
